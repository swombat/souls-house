souls.house — five marks

One scheme across all five: the structure draws in the foreground colour (teal on light, pale on dark — it inherits), and the light is always warm coral, constant in both themes. Someone is home in every one of them. Coral #f15d61 and teal #307c85 are carried over from the current palette, so the room keeps its feel.

A — The lit window

A house at night, one window on. The plainest statement of what the platform is: not a lab, not a deployment target — a house with someone living in it.

C — The dot is a house

The name is a domain, and the domain's dot is where the house lives. In the wordmark the "." between souls and house is a tiny filled house with a lit window. The standalone icon is that same glyph grown up. The most "designed" of the five — the mark and the name are the same joke told twice.
