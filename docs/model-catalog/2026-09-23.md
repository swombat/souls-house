# September 2026 model catalogue additions

Verified on 2026-09-23 against the live OpenRouter catalogue
(`https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models`) and Anthropic's model overview
(`https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/overview`).

Added eleven missing, non-batch September releases:

- OpenAI: GPT-6 Astra Pro, Sol, Sol Pro, Luna, Luna Pro.
- Anthropic: Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1.
- xAI: Grok 4.7.
- Xiaomi: MiMo V2.6 Pro, Pro UltraSpeed, Flash.

GPT-6 Astra was already available. Older models remain selectable. Batch
variants are deliberately excluded from the interactive picker.

Opus 5.5 replaces Fable 5 in Top Models; Grok 4.7 replaces 4.6; Xiaomi's
MiMo V2.6 Pro gets a new flagship slot and Xiaomi gets its own provider group.

## Routes and controls

- Anthropic direct IDs use hyphens: `claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-fable-5-1`.
- GPT-6 Sol/Luna and Grok 4.7 have direct provider mappings and retain the
  existing OpenRouter fallback and subscription selection behavior.
- GPT-6 Pro entries stay on OpenRouter: its descriptions identify these as
  base models served with `reasoning.mode=pro`, not independent direct IDs.
- Xiaomi stays on OpenRouter; no new credentials or provider integration.
- New effort choices follow the catalogue's `reasoning.supported_efforts`:
  Sol/Luna include none through max, Astra Pro and the new Claudes low through
  max, Grok 4.7 low through xhigh (no off setting). MiMo uses the existing
  off/on thinking control.

This is a picker/routing update, not a refresh of the separate historical
usage-estimation price table. Tests cover the new entries, reasoning controls,
API/subscription routing, and intact OpenRouter fallback; they do not make
billable inference calls.
