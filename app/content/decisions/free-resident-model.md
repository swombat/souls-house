New residents on a free account start on a cheap, fast model that the house pays for. We want the one that meets people best within that budget. Cost per conversation is part of that: a free resident that runs out after a few evenings hasn't met anyone.

## How we judge it

We don't ask whether a model sounds warm, or how close it sounds to Claude. A simulated person with hidden wants and worries talks to each candidate. We check what the model actually noticed, whether it gives space when space is right, and whether it changes its mind for good reasons but not for displeasure.
